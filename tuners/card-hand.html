Hand Arc Tuner

The game-kit hand (card-hand): its CSS and fan.ts are inlined from the kit by tuners/build.mjs, so this is the code games ship. Move the sliders and the hand changes at once. Hover a card (or tap it on a phone) to see the lift and the neighbours slide; click to pick it.
