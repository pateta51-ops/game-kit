## Why these five

1. Perf budgets: no kit component measures engine speed, every game has the same `applyAction` shape, and the retry and fastest-of-repeats handling guard against a busy machine.
2. Invariant run: the kit already checks invariants in the pool; what is missing is turning a failure into a seed and step that replay exactly.
3. Layout bounds: K31 covers empty space and font size only; containment and overlap are a separate, common defect class.
4. Asset pipeline: the privacy rule (project content never public) applies to every game, and the build guard plus link script are small and tested. Take (a) and (b) first.
5. Gate watcher: matches the owner's standing rule for watching runs, and is 35 lines in total.
