# Mechanics map

Generated by `node scripts/catalog.mjs` from `docs/mechanics.json` (do not edit). One line per mechanic in the words a planner types; `node kit.mjs search <words>` prints the matching line first. In the source registry, `searchQueries` adds full-query matches without making its individual words aliases. The answer is the part and what to read (a `.md` named alone sits in the part's folder), "partly" with what is missing, or "not in the kit" with its candidate row. "pending: K<n>" means an open row is building the part: it is not in the kit yet. The lines sit under the questions of a plan's game sheet (`sheet` in `docs/mechanics.json`; K584): the sheet has one row per question, and the kit-fit reviewer reads the lines under each.
