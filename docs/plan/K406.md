Every card that leaves a face-down pile turns face up in flight with a real flip, in `deck-motions` (deal, refill turn-over, deck cycle) and `market-row` (refill): the card rotates about its own axis with perspective (foreshortened, never a flat squash), lifts while it turns, swaps face at exactly edge-on with no mirrored face, pop or position jump, and no new card shows face up on the deck before it leaves. A deal from a face-down pile flips by default (a game opts out for a face-up pile). The card-motion tuner shows it and gets sliders for flip lift, perspective and where in the travel the turn happens. Interfaces grow by addition.
