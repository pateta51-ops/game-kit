`effect-library` `glitch`: a broadcast-style burst on a logo or title wordmark. A two-colour split, slices at random positions per burst, dropout, an optional repeat, and a `tear` state (longer, more frames) for victory and defeat. Look in an opt-in `looks/glitch.css`; a small scheduler (`createGlitch(el, opts)`) picks slice positions per burst from a seed. Colours, offsets, frame timing, idle gap, slice count, dropout and repeat chance are settings (CSS variables or options). Its timers stop entirely while motion is off (`speed` 'instant' / reduced motion) and restart when it is back on. `destroy()` clears everything. No game names, fonts or colours in the defaults.
