card-harness `patternProblems`: an opt-in grouping by printed wording. Within a pattern, clauses whose wording key matches (default key: the clause text with numbers masked, words and icon codes kept) must share one record shape; clauses with different words or icons may differ. A hook lets a game excuse a clause (for example a line another line on the same card changes with "instead"), with its reason shown. Passed through `cardsCheckMain`. Without the option, the check is unchanged.
