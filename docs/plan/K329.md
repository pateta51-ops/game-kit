M6: per-actor source visibility at actual takeoff, delivered through effect-library's existing `hideSource: 'step'` policy. Motion-frame supplies `Run.onActorStart` for the first real registered verb of each actor, so cancelled or missing steps do not hide their source and cut-rescheduled steps hide when they actually start. The original `hideSourceUntil: 'start'` proposal uses this canonical API. Manual runs without the optional subscription keep their legacy timeline timing; custom controllers can supply the subscription.
