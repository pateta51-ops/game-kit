motion-frame player, by addition: a beat that ends early (skipped, hurried, snapped, or ended on its fail path such as a missing rect) drops its cues not yet fired, as documented. New player option `onDroppedCue?(cue, beat, why: 'skipped' | 'failed')` is called once per such cue, in timeline order, at the moment the beat ends, so a game can still play the sound. Without the option nothing changes. Every cue of a beat is either fired by onCue or reported by onDroppedCue, exactly once. Tests: skipAll mid-beat reports the unfired cues, not the fired ones; the fail path (missing rect) reports all of the beat's cues; hurry with `hurry: 'keep'` reports none for kept beats; a snapped queued beat reports its cues; no option = old behaviour.
