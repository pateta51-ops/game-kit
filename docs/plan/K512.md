audio-gen tail rule: an opt-in hard cap per category, `tail.maxLengthX` (and/or `tail.maxS`): a one-shot ends at most `maxLengthX` x its spec length after its first sound above `endBelowDbfs`, with a short fade-out (`tail.capFadeMs`, default 60). The manifest records each take's length before and after and whether the cap cut it; the gate flags a take still audible past the cap. Categories without the key keep the rings-out rule.
