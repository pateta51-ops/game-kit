`card-harness` measures test depth. A `Clause` may be marked `conditional` (it has a condition or a cost; the game decides). A clause counts as covered only by a full-resolve test (title names the clause id, not its poor test, body asserts); a conditional clause also needs a `<clause id> poor` test whose body calls `expectNotOffered` or `expectNotFired`. Tests under `.skip`/`.todo` cover nothing. `runInventory` reports both per clause and the conditional count per family; `--check` fails on missing ones; the baseline covers both kinds so a game adopts family by family. New `expectNotOffered(sc, id)` (no option or prompt of `id`, state and events unchanged) and `expectNotFired(sc, id)` (no event of `id`, no prompt, state unchanged), both failing when the effect is offered and then does nothing. Interfaces grow by addition.
